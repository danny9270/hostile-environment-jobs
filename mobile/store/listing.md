## App privacy (App Store) and Data safety (Google Play)
- **Tracking**: No. No ads, no third-party analytics in the app.
- **Data collected, linked to the user's account code, used for App Functionality only**:
  - Purchases (purchase history, via the store and RevenueCat)
  - Identifiers: User ID (the random account code) and the device push token
  - Other data: the job alert preferences the user picks
- **Not collected**: name, email, location, contacts, health, financial info, browsing history, diagnostics.
- **Google Play extras**: data encrypted in transit: Yes. Users can request deletion: Yes (in the app, Your account, Delete my account, or by email).
