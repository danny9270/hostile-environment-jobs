What we publish

  • Your own experience. What happened to you and what you think of it. Good and bad reviews both go up.
  • No names. We take out names of managers, team leaders, colleagues and clients, and anything that points to one person.
  • Nothing that puts people at risk. No exact sites, routes, movement timings, security set-ups, client names or anything under a non-disclosure agreement.
  • No accusations of crimes. If you think a company broke the law or put lives at risk, report it to the right authority or the client. A review isn't the place for it, and we can't publish it.
  • No abuse, and no fakes. We don't publish insults, reviews by a company about itself or its competitors, or more than one review from the same person about the same job.
  • Light edits only. We may trim a review to keep to these rules, but we never change the rating or what it means. If too much would have to go, we ask you to rewrite it.

Are you the company? You can reply to any review, and your reply shows under it. If you think a review is untrue or breaks these rules, email us with a link to it and why. We take it down while we look into it, usually within two working days. We'll never tell you who wrote it.
