How to use the site

A quick walk through everything on Hostile Environment Jobs, filmed on a phone. Searching jobs is free for everyone, and posting a job is free for employers.

  1. Search the jobs

    Tap a trade or region, or type what you're after: a job title, employer, country or qualification.

  2. Open the job

    Read the full details, then apply straight on the employer's own site.

  3. Find a course

    HEAT, EOD, medic and security courses, with reviews from people who went.

  4. Never miss a date

    Course start dates and job closing dates, straight into your phone calendar.

  5. Post a job free

    Fill in the form. We check it and list it, usually within a day.

  6. Know the going rate

    Day rates by role and region, each marked advert, published or estimate.

  7. Build a contractor CV

    UN, NGO or security format. Nothing leaves your phone.

  8. Know the ground

    UK FCDO and US travel advice, local time, visas and recent updates for each country.

  9. Get found

    Add your skills to the talent pool for free. Employers see your trade, not your name.

  10. Alerts on your phone

    Job alerts and the EOD kit list for $6 a year. The rotation tracker is free.

  11. Count down to leave

    The free rotation tracker counts down to your leave and logs the days you work and what you're owed.

  12. Leaving the forces?

    Your resettlement timeline, ELC funding and which courses count.
