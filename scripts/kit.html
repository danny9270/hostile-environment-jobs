EOD team kit list

What a typical humanitarian mine action multi-task team carries: one team leader qualified to IMAS EOD Level 2 and five searchers, two of them trained as intermediate care providers (ICP medics). Use it to check a team's kit, price up a project or see what you'll be working with.

A starting point, not a standard. Every operator, national authority and task is different, so always work to your organisation's SOPs and the national standards. Explosives and detonators are never part of standing team kit: they're drawn from an approved store per task under IMAS 10.50, and this list doesn't cover them. We don't sell kit. Supplier links go to the maker or a dealer; any paid or affiliate link is marked.
