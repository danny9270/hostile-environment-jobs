Privacy

Hostile Environment Jobs (hostileenvironmentjobs.com and the H.E. Jobs app). Questions or deletion requests: contact@hostileenvironmentjobs.com.

The website

We don't use cookies or track you across sites. We count page views with GoatCounter, which stores no cookies and no personal data, so we can see which jobs people look at. If you send a job, we use your name and email only to reply about that job. Job forms are delivered by FormSubmit. If you review a course, we keep it on our own server until we've checked it, show only the name and background you choose, and use your email only to check the review with you. If you subscribe to the Sitrep, we keep your email address with our newsletter provider, Kit, only to send it; every email has an unsubscribe link. When you click through to book a course, the link tells the provider you came from us so they can credit us; it carries no personal data.

The H.E. Jobs app

The app works without a name, email address or password. When you sign up for job alerts we make an account code (like HEJ-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) and keep, on our own server (a Cloudflare Worker and database): the code, the job types, regions, keywords and course types you pick, whether and until when you've paid, and a notification address for each phone you turn alerts on for (up to 5).

  • Payments on the website go through Stripe, which keeps your card details; we keep the email address you give Stripe, so we can help if you lose your code.
  • Payments in the iPhone and Android apps go through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or your Apple or Google account. RevenueCat tells us whether the subscription linked to your account code is active and when it ends.
  • Notifications in the iPhone and Android apps are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (and Apple's push service on iPhone); on the website, by your browser's push service. They get the phone's notification address and the text of each alert, nothing else.
  • The rotation tracker keeps everything on your phone. Nothing in it is sent to us.

We don't sell or share any of this, show adverts in the app or track you across other apps and websites. We use it only to send you the alerts you asked for and run your subscription.

Deleting your account: open the app, go to Your account and tap Delete my account. Your code, choices and phones are deleted from our server straight away. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel the subscription there too, or it keeps renewing. You can also email us to delete it.

The talent pool

If you join the talent pool, we keep your profile, contact details and CV on our own server. Employers we've checked and who pay for search see your profile without your name, contact details or CV; those go to an employer only when you accept their request, and that employer is then responsible for how they use them. Your profile leaves search 90 days after you last confirm you're available and is deleted, with your CV, after 12 months, or straight away when you delete it yourself. If you sign up to search as an employer, we keep your company, name and email to run your account and use your email only to tell you about requests and approvals.

Listings

Searching jobs and posting a job are free. We check listings before they go up but don't employ anyone or vouch for employers: check every role, employer and contract yourself before you travel or sign. Pay figures in the pay guide are indications, not offers.

The app subscription

Job alerts and the full EOD team kit list need a yearly subscription (US$6 a year on the website; the App Store and Google Play show the price in your currency). The rotation tracker is free.

  • A subscription lasts one year and renews automatically each year unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before it ends.
  • In the iPhone app it's charged to your Apple ID and managed or cancelled in your App Store account settings; in the Android app it's charged to your Google account and managed in Google Play, Subscriptions. On the website it's charged to your card through Stripe; email us to stop it renewing.
  • Refunds for store purchases are handled by Apple or Google under their rules. For website payments, email us within 14 days for a refund.
  • Alerts usually arrive within a few hours of a matching listing going up, but notifications depend on your phone, its settings and the push services, so we can't promise every alert arrives or arrives on time. Don't rely on the app as your only source of vacancies.

Kit list, rotation tracker and guides

The kit list, deployment guide and SOP tools are starting points, not standards: always work to your organisation's SOPs, national standards and IMAS. The rotation tracker's pay and day counts are estimates for your own records, not tax or legal advice.
