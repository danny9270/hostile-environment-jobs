First, add it to your home screen

On iPhone, notifications only work once the app is on your home screen (iOS 16.4 or newer).

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open HE Jobs from your home screen and come back to this page.

EOD team kit list

What a typical humanitarian mine action multi-task team carries: a team leader (IMAS EOD Level 2) and five searchers, two of them ICP medics. A starting point, not a standard: always work to your organisation's SOPs and national standards. Explosives and detonators are drawn per task under IMAS 10.50 and aren't covered.
