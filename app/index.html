Job alerts app

New jobs, straight to your phone

Pick the work and regions you want. When a matching job or course goes up on the site, your phone gets a notification within a few hours, wherever you are. No laptop needed and no app store: it installs from this page.

First, add it to your home screen

On iPhone, notifications only work once the app is on your home screen (iOS 16.4 or newer).

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open HE Jobs from your home screen and come back to this page.

Using Chrome on iPhone? Open this page in Safari first.

Alerts work in Chrome straight away, but it's tidier as an app.

Or tap Chrome's ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
